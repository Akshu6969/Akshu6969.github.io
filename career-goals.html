Career Goals & Vision

Bridging the gap between low-level hardware architecture and scalable software platforms.

Short-Term Objectives

My immediate focus is on securing opportunities that allow me to dive deep into embedded systems, computer architecture, and low-level programming. I am actively looking for roles where I can leverage my experience with C, RISC-V assembly, and microcontrollers to write highly efficient, hardware-aware code. Simultaneously, I want to continue building impactful, user-facing tools and platforms that solve real-world logistical problems, streamlining processes from the backend to the user interface.

Core Focus Areas

  • Embedded Systems & Firmware: Designing and debugging systems at the register level, optimizing memory alignment, and pushing the performance limits of microcontrollers and low-level hardware.
  • Platform Optimization & Migration: Applying structural problem-solving to legacy systems, optimizing databases, and ensuring smooth platform migrations without compromising data integrity or user experience.
  • Developer & Student Tooling: Expanding on my experience building intuitive resources—like internship discovery platforms—to create software that empowers other developers and engineers.

Long-Term Vision

Ultimately, my goal is to architect complex systems from the ground up, maintaining a holistic view of the entire technology stack. I want to lead engineering teams in environments where hardware constraints and software scalability intersect—whether that involves designing next-generation computing architectures, developing robust game engines, or building comprehensive enterprise platforms. I aim to be an engineer who not only understands the logic gates and memory registers but can also translate that technical foundation into seamless, high-performance applications.
